About WM Racing

At WM Racing, we hate everything about the hyper-inflated premium mountain bike industry. Traditional carbon and hydroformed alloy frames require complex tooling, carbon layouts, and multi-thousand dollar markups that keep riders locked out of top-tier performance tiers.

Industrial Print Spec Integration

Our modular frames utilize fused additive layer architecture built from custom impact-modified polycarbonate compounds. This gives us direct frame structural engineering flexibility at a tiny fraction of conventional operational scale overhead.

We supply premium custom 3D-printed MTB frames directly to engineers, mechanics, and racing hobbyists globally at disruptive consumer prices. Every single element box configuration is built, reinforced, and optimized right here in our tech production frames loops layout systems.
